  - Post-Panamax container: 366 m by 48.2 m, 15.2 m draught, 179 thousand tonnes displacement.
  - Berthing at 0.15 m/s, as specified. Velocity is squared in the energy, so it carries more of the answer than anything else here.
  - Factors: Cm 1.63 adds the water the ship drags, Ce 0.48 takes away what the ship rotates out, Cs 1.00 and Cc 1.00 take away a little more. Net, the fender sees 78% of the 2018 kNm the ship arrived with.
  - Eccentricity is taken at the quarter point, the usual design assumption. A ship that lands flat on its midships rotates about nothing and hands the fender all of it: Ce becomes 1 and the energy rises by 2.09 times.
  - Abnormal factor 1.50 for a container, taking the design energy from 1573 to 2360 kNm. This is the allowance for the berthing that goes wrong, and it is the largest single term in this calculation.
  - Cone fender, mid grade, 2.00 m: 4000 kNm rated against 2360 kNm required, so it works at 59% of capacity and pushes back 4000 kN.
  - Hull pressure 325 kN/m2 over a 2.8 by 4.4 m panel, within the 400 kN/m2 allowed for this class.
  - Fenders at 16.1 m centres, inside the 20.2 m limit set by vessel length for Feeder. Contact is on the parallel midbody, which is straight, so only the vessel length rule binds; check the end fenders separately with a bow radius.
  - Fender performance here is a scaling model within one family, not a catalogue. Size the fender you are asking for from this, then select against the manufacturer's tested curves at the design temperature, angle and velocity, which is what they guarantee.
