Claims, equations and synthesis come from CoastalWiki, a provenance-first corpus of peer-reviewed coastal engineering. Each entry carries the regime it was established in, because a relation quoted outside its range is the most expensive kind of mistake. Coverage is uneven by design: it records what has been screened, not what exists.
